Privacy Policy

How the Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) handles personal information.

1. Introduction

AGCIH is committed to respecting privacy and handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you visit our website or interact with us online.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only limited information necessary for legitimate institutional purposes, including:

  • Contact details (such as name, email address, organisation) that you voluntarily provide;
  • Information contained in messages or inquiries you send to us;
  • Basic, non-identifying technical data such as browser type, pages visited, and general usage patterns.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected is used solely to:

  • Respond to inquiries, correspondence, or partnership requests;
  • Share publications, updates, or event information where you have chosen to engage;
  • Maintain and improve website functionality and content;
  • Understand aggregated interest in AGCIH’s work for institutional learning purposes.

4. Lawful Basis & Data Minimisation

AGCIH collects and processes personal information on the basis of consent, legitimate institutional interest, or direct engagement initiated by the user. We do not collect unnecessary data and do not engage in profiling, behavioural tracking, or automated decision-making.

5. Cookies & Basic Analytics

This website may use limited cookies or basic analytics tools to understand general site usage. These tools do not identify individual users. You may adjust your browser settings to disable cookies if you prefer.

6. Data Protection & Security

AGCIH applies reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. While we take care to secure information, no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be fully secure.

7. Data Sharing

AGCIH does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Limited information may be shared with trusted service providers supporting website hosting or communications, under appropriate safeguards and only for legitimate operational purposes.

8. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required for legitimate institutional, legal, or accountability reasons.

9. Your Rights & Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by AGCIH, or request that we stop non-essential communications, by contacting us directly.

10. Updates to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in practice or legal context. The most current version will always be published on this page.

11. Contact

For questions or requests relating to this Privacy Policy, contact: admin@agcih.africa.
