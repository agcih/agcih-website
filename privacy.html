Privacy Policy

How the Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) handles personal information.

1. Introduction

Your privacy is important to AGCIH. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect information when you visit our website or interact with us online.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact details such as name, email address and organisation when you voluntarily provide them;
  • Information contained in messages or inquiries you send to us;
  • Non-personal data such as browser type, pages visited and basic usage analytics.

3. How We Use Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and communication;
  • Share updates, publications or event information where you have consented;
  • Improve our website, content and user experience;
  • Compile anonymised statistics to understand interest in our work.

4. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies or basic analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies if you prefer.

5. Data Protection & Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing of Information

AGCIH does not sell or rent personal information to third parties. We may share limited information with trusted service providers who support our website or communications, under appropriate safeguards.

7. Your Choices

You may contact us to update or correct the information we hold about you, or to request that we stop sending you non-essential communications.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

9. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: admin@agcih.africa.
