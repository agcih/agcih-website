Engagement Entry Routes

Three ways institutions typically begin working with AGCIH.

1) Exploratory Conversation

30–45 minutes to understand institutional needs and identify a suitable pathway.

2) Readiness Diagnostic

A structured assessment resulting in priority actions, tools and a practical 30/60/90-day plan.

3) 90-Day Pilot Partnership

Implementation support to operationalise governance gates, controls, monitoring and public trust mechanisms.
