2. Permitted Use (Public Reference)

AGCIH supports responsible public reference to its work. Users may view, download, or print limited copies of AGCIH content for personal, educational, research, or non-commercial use, provided that:

  • AGCIH is clearly acknowledged as the source; and
  • content is not altered, misrepresented, or presented in a misleading context.

3. Quotation & Citation (How to Reference AGCIH)

You may quote short excerpts of AGCIH content for commentary, research, reporting, or policy discussion if you:

  • attribute the excerpt to “Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH)”;

Quotation must not imply AGCIH endorsement of any organisation, product, or political position.

  • mirror, scrape, or systematically extract website content for republication or database creation;

5. Trademarks & Logos

The AGCIH name, logo, and related branding are protected organisational identifiers. They may not be used without written consent. This includes use on websites, reports, proposals, social media, events, or marketing materials.

To request permission to reproduce, adapt, translate, or republish AGCIH content, contact: admin@agcih.africa with (i) the material you wish to use, (ii) intended use, (iii) format, (iv) distribution scope, and (v) whether the use is commercial.

Unauthorised use of AGCIH content may result in takedown requests, withdrawal of permission, public clarification, and/or legal action where appropriate.
