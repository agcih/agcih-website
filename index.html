Who We Are

The Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) is a Zimbabwe-founded research and innovation trust focused on supporting institutions and partners in navigating digital transformation responsibly and effectively.

We work at the intersection of policy, technology and institutional capacity — helping ensure that innovation strengthens public systems, enhances service delivery, and promotes dignity and trust.

Our Focus

  • Responsible Artificial Intelligence (AI) & emerging technology governance
  • Strengthening public systems through digital innovation
  • Secure and ethical data governance practices
  • Knowledge exchange and capacity development for institutions
