About AGCIH

Who we are

The Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Foundation Trust (AGCIH) is an African-led institution committed to governance that feels like compassion, dignity, equality, access, service, honesty, and trust. We believe tomorrow should be better than today, and that institutions must leave legacies the future can be proud to carry.

Founder’s philosophy: Co-creation means we do not decide for communities — we decide with them. What people ask for shapes our work, as long as it leads to meaningful development. Communities are not beneficiaries; they are partners and co-authors of change.

Our approach

Demand-driven. Co-created. Inclusive.

  • Demand-driven: Start from articulated needs; never impose models.
  • Co-creation: Design and implement with those most affected.
  • Inclusion & equity: Women, youth, informal sector, and rural voices are centered.
  • Integrity: We model the accountability we seek in public life.
  • African ownership: Solutions rooted in African realities and leadership.
