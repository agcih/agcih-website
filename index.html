Who We Are

The Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) is a research and innovation institution working at the intersection of technology, development and public systems. We support organisations, learning institutions and public sector actors to adapt to digital transformation in ways that are ethical, inclusive and aligned with human dignity.

Our work focuses on strengthening understanding, capacity and readiness for responsible data use and emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence (AI).

Why This Matters

Digital transformation is reshaping education, health, business and administration. As AI and data systems evolve, organisations and institutions require support to adopt these technologies safely, thoughtfully and in alignment with development goals. AGCIH works to ensure digital advancement strengthens trust, inclusion and opportunities for all.
