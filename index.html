Who We Are

A governance and innovation hub working at the intersection of technology, ethics and the public interest.

The Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) is a policy and innovation think tank supporting African governments, regulators and public institutions to navigate the ethical, social and governance implications of emerging technologies. Our work is grounded in African realities, ethical leadership and the belief that innovation must be governed responsibly to build systems that future generations can trust.
