Who We Are

AGCIH is a Zimbabwe-led, Africa-focused non-profit advancing people-centred governance, digital rights and responsible innovation. We work where institutions, communities and technology meet — so that digital transformation strengthens dignity, participation and trust.

Mission

To co-create governance solutions that empower citizens, strengthen institutions and shape ethical digital ecosystems — with a focus on youth, women and marginalised groups.

Strategic Focus

  • Digital Rights & Safety: literacy, online harm reporting and citizen protection.
  • Data Protection & Compliance: DPO support, audits and toolkits for the Data Protection Act.
  • Responsible AI & Public Policy Dialogue: national dialogues and policy briefs before frameworks harden.

SDG Alignment

  • SDG 16 – Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions
  • SDG 5 – Gender Equality
  • SDG 10 – Reduced Inequalities
  • SDG 17 – Partnerships
