Who We Are

The Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) is a Zimbabwe-led, Africa-focused institution advancing transparent, inclusive and people-centred governance. We equip communities, public institutions and civic actors with the knowledge, tools and **ethical AI practices** needed to build accountable systems.

Anchor principle: AI must serve people — and strengthen development, not replace human purpose.

Strategic Focus

  • Civic Empowerment & Voice — co-creation platforms, listening labs, participation tools.
  • Good Governance & Rule of Law — anti-corruption, accountability, service delivery improvements.
  • Digital Rights & AI Governance — data protection compliance, responsible AI, public trust.

SDG Alignment

  • SDG 16: Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions
  • SDG 5: Gender Equality & Inclusion
  • SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities
  • SDG 17: Partnerships for the Goals

Why It Matters

Digital transformation is accelerating. Without safeguards and participation, AI and data systems can deepen inequality. AGCIH ensures technology, governance reform and civic action work together to protect rights, expand opportunity and build resilient societies in Zimbabwe and across Africa.

Who We Are

The Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) is an independent non-profit organisation advancing transparent, inclusive and people-centered governance across Africa. We provide civic actors, institutions and communities with the tools, knowledge and digital capacity to shape accountable public systems.

We work to ensure governance feels like service, protection and dignity — where citizens are heard, institutions are trusted, and technology is used ethically and responsibly.

Our Mission

To strengthen democratic governance ecosystems by promoting civic participation, ethical digital innovation, and accountable institutions — with a focus on youth, women, and marginalized groups.

Strategic Focus Areas

  • Civic Empowerment & Voice: Community-based engagement, leadership strengthening, public dialogue and participation platforms.
  • Good Governance & Rule of Law: Anti-corruption advocacy, institutional support, accountability frameworks and public oversight.
  • Digital Innovation for Democracy: Ethical AI, public data governance, civic-tech solutions and digital rights protection.

Why Our Work Matters

Africa is entering a new era where governance, democracy and digital transformation intersect. Yet, without safeguards, capacity and inclusion, these advancements may deepen inequality.

AGCIH ensures technology, governance reform and civic action work together to protect rights, expand opportunities and build resilient societies.

We root our work in African realities, community priorities, and global democratic values.

SDG Alignment

Our work directly contributes to:

  • SDG 16: Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions
  • SDG 5: Gender Equality & Inclusion
  • SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities
  • SDG 17: Partnerships for Sustainable Development
