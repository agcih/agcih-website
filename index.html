Who We Are

The Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) is a Zimbabwe-founded policy and innovation hub working at the intersection of governance, technology, and public interest. We support institutions and communities to navigate digital transformation in ways that protect dignity, prevent harm, and enable equitable participation in the emerging AI-driven world.

Our Role

  • Generate clear knowledge on AI and emerging technologies
  • Strengthen institutional and community digital readiness
  • Support ethical implementation of digital systems
  • Convene collaboration across government, academia & innovators
