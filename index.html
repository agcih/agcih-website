Who We Are

The Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) is a Zimbabwe-founded policy research and innovation hub supporting responsible and inclusive digital transformation. We work at the intersection of governance, technology and public interest to ensure that the adoption of AI and emerging technologies strengthens people, institutions and development.

Our Work

  • Understanding and communicating emerging technology impacts
  • Strengthening institutional capacity and digital readiness
  • Supporting ethical implementation of digital systems
  • Convening multi-sector knowledge and collaboration spaces
