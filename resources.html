Core Themes

AGCIH resources are organised into themes that support institutional readiness, operational governance, and public trust.

AI Governance & Institutional Readiness

Governance readiness diagnostics and institutional design guidance — clarifying mandates, decision rights, risk escalation, and oversight pathways for AI-enabled public systems.

Digital Governance–Risk–Compliance (GRC)

Practical approaches to governance, risk, and compliance in digital transformation — strengthening integrity, accountability, and controls for automated decision systems.

Digital Transformation & Institutional Resilience

Guidance on building resilient, coherent governance arrangements that support safe implementation of digital systems, including data governance and institutional coordination.

Public Trust, Inclusion & Accountability

Governance approaches that protect rights and strengthen legitimacy — ensuring AI-enabled public services operate with accountability, transparency, and public confidence.
