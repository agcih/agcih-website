Themes & Insight Series

Our emerging work is organised into themes that will shape AGCIH’s publications and tools.

AI Governance & Institutional Readiness

How public institutions can prepare for AI – from governance frameworks and risk assessments to leadership, mandates and oversight in key sectors such as justice, health and social protection.

Digital GRC & Public Sector Integrity

Integrating Governance–Risk–Compliance into digital transformation, linking anti-corruption, accountability and algorithmic decision-making across state institutions.

Digital Transformation & Sovereign Futures

Exploring how infrastructure, data, platforms and innovation ecosystems can support a sovereign, inclusive digital future for Zimbabwe and the wider region.

Civic Innovation, Rights & Inclusion

Work at the intersection of digital rights, gender equality, youth engagement and civic technology – ensuring Africa’s digital decade remains people-centred.
