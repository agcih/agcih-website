Our Team

AGCIH is led by a Founder and Executive Director and guided by an independent Board of Trustees providing strategic direction and fiduciary oversight across governance, gender equality, digital transformation, and public sector innovation.

To understand how the Board functions and the principles that guide our work, read our Governance and Board Charter.

Leadership

The Office of the Executive Director provides overall strategic leadership for AGCIH.

Danai Hazel Kudya

Founder and Executive Director, Africa Governance and Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH)

Danai Hazel Kudya is the founder of the Africa Governance and Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH), an independent governance institution working at the intersection of public law, institutional design, and AI implementation across Africa. She established AGCIH to fill a precise institutional gap: the absence of a dedicated African governance body capable of operationalising accountability for high impact digital and AI enabled public systems.

With more than 15 years of experience in governance, institutional strengthening, and public interest accountability, Danai brings a practitioner's depth to AGCIH's mission. Her career has spanned independent constitutional commissions, United Nations entities, and civil society, including the leadership of complex, multi stakeholder programmes in demanding political and operational environments across Zimbabwe and the continent. That range informs the practical, implementation grounded approach that distinguishes AGCIH's work.

As an institutional architect and original thinker in African AI governance, Danai has developed a body of doctrinal work that positions AGCIH as a substantive voice ahead of the field. Her frameworks, including the Continuous Administration doctrine, the administrative risk and institutional absorption concepts, and AGCIH's Procurement Governance Matrix, address the governance gaps that arise when AI systems enter public administration without adequate oversight architecture.

Her engagement spans government ministries, regulatory authorities, and international partners, with a focus on building the institutional capacity that makes responsible AI implementation possible. At AGCIH, her strategic leadership translates long standing expertise in public law and governance reform into the frameworks, standards, and accountability mechanisms that digital era public institutions require.

Board of Trustees

The Board of Trustees provides independent oversight and strategic guidance, ensuring that AGCIH's work remains rooted in strong governance, inclusion, ethics, and public interest outcomes.

Ms. Shimtah Atipa Nembaware

Trustee, Gender, Inclusion and Equity

Manager, Complaints Handling and Investigations, Zimbabwe Gender Commission

Ms. Shimtah Atipa Nembaware is a Trustee of the Africa Governance and Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH), where she holds the gender, inclusion, and equity mandate on the Board. She brings to this role more than a decade of professional experience as a gender equality and human rights practitioner, with substantive expertise in institutional investigations, rights based community programming, and the structural mechanisms of accountability.

In her principal professional capacity, Ms. Nembaware serves as Manager, Complaints Handling and Investigations at the Zimbabwe Gender Commission, a constitutional body mandated to advance gender equality and equity in Zimbabwe. In this role, she oversees processes of redress, institutional accountability, and systemic response to gender based harms, including those emerging in digital and technology mediated environments.

Within AGCIH, Ms. Nembaware provides strategic guidance on gender responsive governance and safeguarding as the institution develops practical tools and accountability frameworks for responsible AI deployment and public sector digital innovation. She helps ensure that AGCIH's governance instruments remain attentive to the differential impacts of AI on women, marginalised communities, and vulnerable populations, and that inclusion is embedded as a structural principle in the institution's work.

Ms. Mirirai Muyengwa Mapasure

Trustee, Digital Transformation and AI Governance

Data Scientist and IT Consultant, Public Health and ICT

Ms. Mirirai Muyengwa Mapasure is a Data Scientist and IT Consultant with extensive experience across ICT and public health. She brings a blend of technical expertise, data driven decision making, and strategic advisory work in government and development settings.

Within AGCIH, she provides strategic guidance on digital transformation, AI adoption, and data governance, supporting a governance led approach to technology choices that is ethical, inclusive, and context aware.

Ms. Tinah Kadhau

Trustee, Governance, Legal Compliance and Risk

Ms. Tinah Kadhau is a Board Member of the Africa Governance and Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH), where she provides independent governance oversight across the institution's legal compliance, internal governance architecture, and organisational risk management functions.

In her board capacity, Ms. Kadhau brings critical legal and institutional acuity to AGCIH's governance framework. She helps ensure that the institution's policies, decision making structures, and operational conduct remain aligned with applicable legal standards and sound ethical principles, a mandate that is foundational to AGCIH's credibility as an independent governance institution.

Ms. Kadhau plays a central role in strengthening AGCIH's internal controls, supporting the development of clear and accountable decision making processes, and ensuring that the institution's risk posture is actively managed and proportionate to its mandate. Her oversight extends to the governance of AGCIH's institutional instruments, including its policies, frameworks, and compliance obligations.

Mrs. Catherine Mareya Chiviya

Trustee, Finance, Audit and Risk Oversight

Mrs. Catherine Mareya Chiviya is a Trustee of the Africa Governance and Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH), where she holds responsibility for financial governance, audit oversight, and organisational risk stewardship. She brings to the Board a rigorous commitment to transparent financial systems, sound resource management, and the institutional accountability frameworks that underpin credibility in the public and development sectors.

In her trustee capacity, Mrs. Mareya Chiviya helps ensure that AGCIH maintains strong financial controls and that its resource management practices are aligned with the standards expected of an independent governance institution operating across multiple stakeholder environments. Her oversight extends to audit readiness, financial reporting integrity, and the responsible stewardship of organisational assets, functions that are essential to sustaining the confidence of AGCIH's partners, funders, and the institutions it engages.

Mrs. Mareya Chiviya brings a practitioner's understanding of how financial governance underpins institutional resilience. Her work on the Board reflects a recognition that governance credibility is inseparable from financial discipline and that institutions which seek to hold others accountable must themselves maintain demonstrably sound internal systems.
