Our Team

AGCIH is led by a Founder & Executive Director and guided by an independent Board of Trustees providing strategic direction and fiduciary oversight across governance, gender equality, digital transformation, and public-sector innovation.

To understand how the Board functions and the principles that guide our work, read our Governance & Board Charter.

Leadership

The Office of the Executive Director provides overall strategic leadership for AGCIH.

Danai Hazel Kudya

Founder & Executive Director – Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH)

Danai Hazel Kudya is a governance and rule of law specialist with more than 15 years’ experience supporting institutional strengthening, accountability, and public-interest governance in Zimbabwe and across Africa. Her work has spanned independent commissions, UN entities, and civil society, including leadership of complex multi-stakeholder programmes in demanding political and operational environments.

At AGCIH, she provides strategic leadership grounded in governance discipline and implementation experience, applying long-standing expertise in public law, institutional accountability, and governance reform to high-impact digital and AI-enabled public systems. Her focus is practical: strengthening mandates, oversight pathways, and governance readiness so institutions can move from ambition to responsible, trusted implementation.

Board of Trustees

The Board of Trustees provides independent oversight and strategic guidance, ensuring that AGCIH’s work remains rooted in strong governance, inclusion, ethics, and public interest outcomes.

Ms. Shimtah Atipa Nembaware

Trustee – Gender, Inclusion & Equity

Manager, Complaints Handling & Investigations – Zimbabwe Gender Commission

Ms. Shimtah Atipa Nembaware is an experienced gender equality and human rights practitioner with over a decade of work spanning investigations, community programming, and rights-based institutional response. She currently serves as Manager, Complaints Handling & Investigations at the Zimbabwe Gender Commission, supporting national efforts to strengthen prevention, redress, and institutional accountability relating to gender-based harms, including those arising in digital environments.

Within AGCIH, she provides strategic guidance on gender-responsive governance, safeguarding, and inclusion as the Hub develops practical tools for responsible AI and public-sector innovation.

Ms. Mirirai Muyengwa Mapasure

Trustee – Digital Transformation & AI Governance

Data Scientist & IT Consultant – Public Health & ICT

Ms. Mirirai Muyengwa Mapasure is a Data Scientist and IT Consultant with extensive experience across ICT and public health. She brings a blend of technical expertise, data-driven decision-making, and strategic advisory work in government and development settings.

Within AGCIH, she provides strategic guidance on digital transformation, AI adoption, and data governance, supporting a governance-led approach to technology choices that is ethical, inclusive, and context-aware.
