Our Team

AGCIH is led by a Founder and Executive Director and guided by an independent Board of Trustees providing strategic direction and fiduciary oversight across governance, gender equality, digital transformation, and public sector innovation.

To understand how the Board functions and the principles that guide our work, read our Governance and Board Charter.

Leadership

The Office of the Executive Director provides overall strategic leadership for AGCIH.

Danai Hazel Kudya

Founder and Executive Director, Africa Governance and Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH)

Danai Hazel Kudya is the Founder and Executive Director of AGCIH, an independent institution working at the intersection of public law, institutional design, and responsible AI implementation in Africa.

With more than 15 years of experience across constitutional commissions, United Nations entities, and civil society, she brings deep expertise in governance, accountability, and institutional reform.

At AGCIH, she provides strategic leadership on governance frameworks, public sector readiness, and accountability systems for digital and AI enabled public institutions.

Board of Trustees

The Board of Trustees provides independent oversight and strategic guidance, ensuring that AGCIH's work remains rooted in strong governance, inclusion, ethics, and public interest outcomes.

Ms. Shimtah Atipa Nembaware

Trustee, Gender, Inclusion and Equity

Manager, Complaints Handling and Investigations, Zimbabwe Gender Commission

Ms. Nembaware is a gender equality and human rights practitioner with more than a decade of experience in investigations, community programming, and institutional accountability.

At AGCIH, she provides strategic guidance on gender responsive governance, safeguarding, and inclusion in responsible AI and public sector digital innovation.

Ms. Mirirai Muyengwa Mapasure

Trustee, Digital Transformation and AI Governance

Data Scientist and IT Consultant, Public Health and ICT

Ms. Mapasure is a Data Scientist and IT Consultant with experience across ICT and public health.

At AGCIH, she provides guidance on digital transformation, AI adoption, and data governance, supporting ethical and context aware technology choices.

Ms. Tinah Kadhau

Trustee, Governance, Legal Compliance and Risk

Ms. Kadhau provides Board oversight on legal compliance, internal governance, and organisational risk management.

She supports AGCIH in maintaining sound internal controls, accountable decision making, and alignment with applicable legal and ethical standards.

Mrs. Catherine Mareya Chiviya

Trustee, Finance, Audit and Risk Oversight

Mrs. Mareya Chiviya provides oversight on financial governance, audit readiness, and organisational risk stewardship.
