Our Team

AGCIH is led by a Founder and Executive Director and guided by an independent Board of Trustees providing strategic direction, fiduciary oversight, and governance stewardship across the institution’s work.

To understand how the Board functions and the principles that guide our work, read our Governance and Board Charter.

Leadership

Founder and Executive Director

Danai Hazel Kudya

Danai Hazel Kudya is the Founder and Executive Director of AGCIH, an independent institution working at the intersection of public law, institutional design, and responsible AI implementation in Africa.

With more than 15 years of experience across constitutional commissions, United Nations entities, and civil society, she provides strategic leadership on governance frameworks, institutional readiness, and accountability systems for digital and AI-enabled public institutions.
