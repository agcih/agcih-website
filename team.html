Our Team

AGCIH is led by a Founder & Executive Director and guided by an independent Board of Trustees providing strategic direction and fiduciary oversight across governance, gender equality, digital transformation, and public-sector innovation.

To understand how the Board functions and the principles that guide our work, read our Governance & Board Charter.

Leadership

The Office of the Executive Director provides overall strategic leadership for AGCIH.

Danai Hazel Kudya

Founder & Executive Director – Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH)

Danai Hazel Kudya is a governance and rule of law specialist with more than 15 years’ experience supporting institutional strengthening, accountability, and public-interest governance in Zimbabwe and across Africa. Her work has spanned independent commissions, UN entities, and civil society, including leadership of complex multi-stakeholder programmes in demanding political and operational environments.

At AGCIH, she provides strategic leadership grounded in governance discipline and implementation experience, applying long-standing expertise in public law, institutional accountability, and governance reform to high-impact digital and AI-enabled public systems. Her focus is practical: strengthening mandates, oversight pathways, and governance readiness so institutions can move from ambition to responsible, trusted implementation.
