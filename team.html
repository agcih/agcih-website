Our Team

AGCIH is led by a Founder and Executive Director and guided by an independent Board of Trustees providing strategic direction and fiduciary oversight across governance, gender equality, digital transformation, and public sector innovation.

To understand how the Board functions and the principles that guide our work, read our Governance and Board Charter.

Leadership

The Office of the Executive Director provides overall strategic leadership for AGCIH.

Danai Hazel Kudya

Founder and Executive Director, Africa Governance and Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH)

Danai Hazel Kudya is the founder of the Africa Governance and Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH), an independent governance institution working at the intersection of public law, institutional design, and AI implementation across Africa. She established AGCIH to fill a precise institutional gap: the absence of a dedicated African governance body capable of operationalising accountability for high impact digital and AI enabled public systems.

With more than 15 years of experience in governance, institutional strengthening, and public interest accountability, Danai brings a practitioner's depth to AGCIH's mission. Her career has spanned independent constitutional commissions, United Nations entities, and civil society, including the leadership of complex, multi stakeholder programmes in demanding political and operational environments across Zimbabwe and the continent. That range informs the practical, implementation grounded approach that distinguishes AGCIH's work.

As an institutional architect and original thinker in African AI governance, Danai has developed a body of doctrinal work that positions AGCIH as a substantive voice ahead of the field. Her frameworks, including the Continuous Administration doctrine, the administrative risk and institutional absorption concepts, and AGCIH's Procurement Governance Matrix, address the governance gaps that arise when AI systems enter public administration without adequate oversight architecture.

Her engagement spans government ministries, regulatory authorities, and international partners, with a focus on building the institutional capacity that makes responsible AI implementation possible. At AGCIH, her strategic leadership translates long standing expertise in public law and governance reform into the frameworks, standards, and accountability mechanisms that digital era public institutions require.

Board of Trustees

The Board of Trustees provides independent oversight and strategic guidance, ensuring that AGCIH’s work remains rooted in strong governance, inclusion, ethics, and public interest outcomes.

Ms. Shimtah Atipa Nembaware

Trustee, Gender, Inclusion and Equity

Manager, Complaints Handling and Investigations, Zimbabwe Gender Commission

Ms. Shimtah Atipa Nembaware is a Trustee of the Africa Governance and Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH), where she holds the gender, inclusion, and equity mandate on the Board. She brings to this role more than a decade of professional experience as a gender equality and human rights practitioner, with substantive expertise in institutional investigations, rights based community programming, and the structural mechanisms of accountability.

In her principal professional capacity, Ms. Nembaware serves as Manager, Complaints Handling and Investigations at the Zimbabwe Gender Commission, a constitutional body mandated to advance gender equality and equity in Zimbabwe. In this role, she oversees processes of redress, institutional accountability, and systemic response to gender based harms, including those emerging in digital and technology mediated environments.
