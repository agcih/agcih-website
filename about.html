Who We Are

The Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) is a policy research and innovation trust advancing ethical digital governance and institutional readiness in Zimbabwe and across Africa.

We support public institutions, regulatory bodies, academia and partners to design and implement practical and responsible approaches to emerging technologies — particularly Artificial Intelligence (AI) and data governance.

Our Vision

To support Africa’s transition into a future where technology strengthens dignity, equity and public trust in governance.

Our Mission

To foster ethical, inclusive and effective digital transformation by strengthening institutional capacity, research, and innovation collaboration.
