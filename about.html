About AGCIH

AGCIH is an independent non-profit institution dedicated to people-centred governance and responsible innovation. We work at the intersection of civic participation, accountability and **AI governance**, ensuring digital transformation advances dignity and development.

Vision

An ethical and inclusive Africa where governance is rooted in compassion, equity, service and trust — and where technology strengthens human dignity.

Mission

To strengthen democratic ecosystems by promoting civic participation, ethical digital innovation and accountable institutions — with a focus on youth, women and marginalised groups.

Values

  • Dignity: every voice matters.
  • Co-creation: solutions with communities, not for them.
  • Integrity: transparency and accountability.
  • Inclusion: leave no one behind in the digital era.
  • Stewardship: responsible, human-centred use of AI.

Founder & Executive Director

Danai Hazel Kudya is a governance and human rights practitioner specialising in civic participation, accountability, digital rights and emerging technology governance. Her work bridges institutions and communities, ensuring AI and data systems are governed with dignity, equity and public trust.

Signature principle: AI must serve people — not replace them, exploit them or disregard them. Technology must advance human development, dignity and equality.
