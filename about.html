Who We Are

The Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) is a policy innovation and research trust that supports governments, public institutions and partners in building responsible, inclusive and future-ready governance systems in the era of artificial intelligence (AI) and digital transformation.

We focus on evidence, foresight and applied innovation—helping institutions design and implement digital practices that improve reliability and strengthen public confidence in service delivery.

Our approach is collaborative, non-partisan and aligned to national priorities (Smart Zimbabwe 2030, NDS1, and the SDGs).

Our Vision

To be Africa’s leading hub for ethical innovation and digital governance excellence, supporting institutions to harness technology for inclusive, transparent and sustainable development.

Our Mission

To strengthen policy ecosystems, institutional capacity and innovation partnerships so that technology serves the public good, enhances service delivery and accelerates progress towards national and global development goals.

How We Work

  • Policy research & evidence that informs decision-making.
  • Innovation advisory & institutional support for safe, practical adoption.
  • Technical dialogue & collaboration across sectors.
  • Foresight & pilot testing of responsible, context-appropriate solutions.

We combine technical understanding with governance insight to ensure digital transformation remains ethical, efficient and inclusive.
