About AGCIH

AGCIH is an independent non-profit institution dedicated to people-centred governance and responsible innovation. We work at the intersection of civic participation, accountability and **AI governance**, ensuring digital transformation advances dignity and development.

Vision

An ethical and inclusive Africa where governance is rooted in compassion, equity, service and trust — and where technology strengthens human dignity.

Mission

To strengthen democratic ecosystems by promoting civic participation, ethical digital innovation and accountable institutions — with a focus on youth, women and marginalised groups.

Values

  • Dignity: every voice matters.
  • Co-creation: solutions with communities, not for them.
  • Integrity: transparency and accountability.
  • Inclusion: leave no one behind in the digital era.
  • Stewardship: responsible, human-centred use of AI.

Founder & Executive Director

Danai Hazel Kudya is a governance and human rights practitioner specialising in civic participation, accountability, digital rights and emerging technology governance. Her work bridges institutions and communities, ensuring AI and data systems are governed with dignity, equity and public trust.

Signature principle: AI must serve people — not replace them, exploit them or disregard them. Technology must advance human development, dignity and equality.

Board

A five-member Board provides strategic oversight and fiduciary responsibility. Profiles will be published upon confirmation.

Who We Are

Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) advances dignity-centred governance, digital rights and responsible innovation. We work where institutions, communities and technology meet — so that digital transformation strengthens participation, accountability and public trust.

Why We Exist

Zimbabwe’s Data Protection Act is in force, but a national AI governance architecture is still emerging. This is a rare window to design ethical, people-centred systems before frameworks harden. AGCIH helps shape that future with communities and institutions — not over them.

Vision

A just, ethical and inclusive Africa where governance is rooted in compassion, equity, service and trust.

Mission

To co-create people-centred governance through technology, data rights and civic innovation — with a focus on youth, women and marginalised communities.

Our Approach

  • Co-creation, not imposition: communities are partners from design to delivery.
  • Constructive collaboration: we work with ministries and regulators to enable reform.
  • Evidence & practice: compliance toolkits, audits and policy briefs grounded in realities.
  • Future-ready: preparing society for AI and digital transition with safeguards.

Strategic Focus

  • Digital Rights & Online Safety — literacy, resilience and citizen protection.
  • Data Protection & Compliance — DPO support, audits and governance toolkits.
  • Responsible AI & Policy Dialogue — national dialogues and briefs before regulation sets.
