About AGCIH

Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) is a Zimbabwe-led and Africa-focused institution advancing people-centered governance, digital rights and responsible innovation. We work to build societies where institutions are accountable, citizens are empowered, and technology strengthens dignity and justice.

Our Vision

A just, ethical and inclusive Africa where governance is rooted in compassion, equity, service and trust.

Our Mission

To co-create governance solutions that empower citizens, strengthen institutions and shape ethical digital ecosystems.

Our Approach

  • Co-creation with communities, not top-down solutions
  • Constructive collaboration with government and regulators
  • Grounded in African realities and global democratic values
  • Future-oriented — preparing society for AI and digital transition
