Who We Are

AGCIH is a policy innovation and research hub supporting responsible, inclusive and future-ready governance systems in the era of artificial intelligence and digital transformation. Our work strengthens public trust, institutional capability and ethical technology adoption.

Vision

To be Africa’s leading hub for ethical innovation and digital governance excellence, supporting institutions to harness technology for inclusive and sustainable development.

Mission

To strengthen policy ecosystems, institutional preparedness and collaboration networks so that technology serves public good and accelerates national development goals.
