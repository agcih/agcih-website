Who We Are

Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) advances dignity-centred governance, digital rights and responsible innovation across Africa. We partner with communities, government and civic actors to build accountable institutions and inclusive public policy.

Vision

A just, ethical and inclusive Africa where governance is rooted in compassion, equity, service and trust.

Mission

To co-create people-centred governance through technology, data rights and civic innovation.

Our Distinctives

  • First-mover: Zimbabwe lacks an AI Commission; we help shape frameworks now.
  • Co-creation: demand-driven solutions with communities — never impositional.
  • Constructive posture: we work with ministries and regulators to enable reform.
  • Evidence-based: policy briefs, compliance audits and an annual State of Digital Rights & AI report.
