What We Do

Supporting Africa’s digital transformation through ethical, governance-led approaches.

AGCIH works through three interconnected programme pillars that respond to Africa’s urgent need for responsible AI governance, digital transformation readiness and civic innovation.

Responsible AI & Data Governance Lab

Helping institutions adopt AI and data-driven tools safely, ethically and with accountability.

Our Responsible AI & Data Governance Lab develops practical frameworks and tools that support lawful, safe and rights-respecting use of AI in public institutions.

  • AI governance frameworks and policy guidance
  • Algorithmic impact assessments and risk analysis
  • Ethics guidelines and safeguards for AI deployment
  • Data protection and privacy alignment support
  • Capacity building for policymakers and regulators

Who We Support

Ministries, national commissions, regulators, data protection authorities, oversight bodies and public institutions implementing AI or advanced data systems in justice, health, education, administration or public services.

Digital Governance & Public Sector Innovation

Strengthening institutional capacity for sustainable, accountable digital transformation.

Digital transformation is not only about technology — it is a governance and institutional reform process. AGCIH helps public institutions build the foundations for effective digital governance.

  • Digital transformation readiness assessments
  • Governance frameworks for emerging technologies
  • Leadership and capacity building for public officials
  • Public sector innovation advisory
  • Support for inter-ministerial coordination and digital strategies

Our Approach

We ensure digital tools are supported by strong mandates, processes, skills, safeguards and accountability mechanisms. Technology must reinforce legitimacy, trust and inclusion — not undermine them.

Policy Research, Foresight & Civic Innovation

Providing evidence, futures thinking and civic technology models for inclusive digital governance.

AGCIH undertakes research and foresight work to help institutions understand and navigate emerging governance challenges linked to AI, digitalisation and societal change.

  • Policy briefs and thematic research papers
  • Scenario planning and foresight studies
  • Civic technology tools and innovation models
  • Digital rights and inclusion assessments

Why It Matters

Africa’s digital future must be people-centred. Our civic innovation work ensures that policies, systems and digital tools remain inclusive, ethical and responsive to real community needs.
