What We Do

AGCIH is a governance and civic innovation hub working to ensure that digital transformation and AI in Zimbabwe and Africa are developed and implemented in ways that protect dignity, prevent harm and expand opportunity. We support the responsible use of AI and emerging technologies, and help shape national and institutional governance approaches so that technology strengthens communities rather than leaving them behind.

Our distinct role is to connect governance with technology, technology with dignity, and institutions with communities.

Our Pillars

1) Knowledge & Research on Emerging Technologies

Making the complex understandable. We produce clear, contextual insights that help decision-makers, media, communities and institutions understand AI and digital transformation.

  • Research briefs on digital transformation and public systems
  • Policy and regulatory analysis on AI, data governance and ethics
  • Translation of global good practice into Zimbabwean/African context
  • Baseline studies on awareness and readiness across sectors

2) Institutional Capacity & Skills Development

Preparing systems and people for the digital future. Executive short courses and practical learning for organisations and learning environments.

  • Understanding AI and emerging technologies
  • Responsible digital transformation practices
  • Data protection & information management fundamentals
  • Digital literacy and critical use skills

3) Public Interest Technology Advisory

Supporting ethical adoption and implementation. Technical advisory to integrate digital systems, AI and data processes in safe, compliant and inclusive ways.

  • Alignment with the Cyber and Data Protection Act
  • Internal AI and data governance safeguards
  • Ethical use guidance for digital tools and workflows
  • Practical, steady, sustainable implementation support

4) Dialogue, Collaboration & Knowledge Exchange

Creating shared understanding and collective direction. Neutral platforms for government, technologists, academia, private sector and communities.

  • Roundtables and expert dialogues
  • Policy & innovation forums; communities of practice
  • Collaborative research and pilot learning projects
  • Reducing fear, fragmentation and misinformation

Our Ethos

Governance must feel like compassion. Technology must serve dignity. Institutions must listen to the people they exist to serve.

Our work prevents manipulation, exclusion and new forms of inequality that arise when technology moves faster than public awareness and safeguards. We aim to be a catalyst within the AI governance and policy ecosystem, working with the Ministry of ICT, POTRAZ, academia and partners to build trust, capacity and relevant national frameworks.
