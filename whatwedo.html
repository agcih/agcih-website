What We Do

Supporting Africa’s digital transformation through ethical, governance-led approaches.

AGCIH works through three interconnected programme pillars that respond to the continent’s need for responsible AI governance, digital transformation readiness and civic innovation.

Responsible AI & Data Governance Lab

Helping institutions adopt AI and data-driven tools safely, ethically and with accountability.

Our Responsible AI & Data Governance Lab focuses on practical frameworks and tools that support safe, lawful and rights-respecting use of AI in public institutions.

  • AI governance frameworks and policy guidance
  • Algorithmic impact assessments and risk analysis
  • Ethics guidelines and safeguards for AI deployment
  • Data protection and privacy alignment support
  • Capacity building for policymakers and regulators

Who We Support

Ministries, national commissions, regulators, national data protection authorities, oversight bodies and public institutions implementing AI or advanced data systems.

Digital Governance & Public Sector Innovation

Strengthening institutional capacity for sustainable, accountable digital transformation.

Digital transformation is not only about technology; it is a governance and institutional reform process. AGCIH helps public institutions build the foundations for effective digital governance.

  • Digital transformation readiness assessments
  • Governance frameworks for new technologies
  • Leadership and capacity-building for public officials
  • Public sector innovation advisory
  • Support for cross-government coordination and strategy design

Our Approach

We ensure digital tools are supported by mandates, processes, skills, safeguards and accountability mechanisms. Technology must reinforce legitimacy.

Policy Research, Foresight & Civic Innovation

Providing evidence, futures thinking and civic models for inclusive digital governance.

AGCIH undertakes research and policy analysis to help institutions anticipate and navigate emerging governance challenges linked to AI and digitalisation.

  • Policy briefs and thematic research papers
  • Scenario planning and foresight studies
  • Civic technology tools and models
  • Digital rights and inclusion analysis

Why It Matters

Digitalisation must be people-centred. Our civic innovation work ensures policies, systems and tools remain inclusive, ethical and accessible for all communities.
