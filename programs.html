1. Digital Rights & Online Safety

We build digital literacy and online protection skills, especially among youth, women, journalists and educators. This includes training academies, awareness campaigns, and a citizen helpdesk to report digital harm.

2. Data Protection & Institutional Compliance

We support organisations to comply with the Data Protection Act — including DPO training, governance audits, policy templates and secure data management support.

3. Responsible Artificial Intelligence & Public Policy Dialogue

We convene national dialogues, produce policy briefs, and build local expertise to shape ethical AI adoption in public and community systems before frameworks are formalised.

4. Youth Civic Innovation Lab

A participatory space where young Africans co-create digital and governance solutions rooted in community needs and lived experiences.
