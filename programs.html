Research, Foresight & Policy Innovation

Evidence-based studies on digital transformation and AI readiness; futures scanning; and concise knowledge products that inform planning and responsible adoption across sectors (on request; non-advocacy).

Implementation support aligned with Zimbabwe’s Data Protection Act and emerging standards: data stewardship basics, AI use protocols, and organisational safeguards that prioritise dignity, safety and trust.

Digital Rights & Civic Resilience

We work to ensure that individuals and communities understand, defend and exercise their digital rights. This includes strengthening public awareness, promoting safe online spaces, and advancing protections for groups most affected by digital exclusion or harm.

Data Protection & Governance Support

We promote responsible data governance through policy engagement, institutional capacity support and awareness on privacy, rights and compliance. Our work contributes to building systems that protect dignity and foster public trust.

Responsible AI & Public Interest Innovation

We convene dialogues and collaborate with institutions, communities and researchers to shape ethical, inclusive and future-oriented approaches to emerging technologies. Our focus is on ensuring innovation strengthens human rights and public accountability.

1. Digital Rights, Literacy & Online Safety

We strengthen public awareness and protection of digital rights — especially among young people, women and civic actors. Through trainings, safe-space chats, public campaigns and a support referral network, we help citizens understand and defend their digital dignity.

Key Activities:
  • Digital literacy and civic tech training labs
  • Online safety workshops for youth & activists
  • Rapid response referral network for online harm
SDGs: 16, 5, 10

2. Data Protection Compliance & Institutional Support

With Zimbabwe’s Data Protection Act now operational, institutions require guidance. We provide practical, trust-building compliance pathways that protect citizens, reduce risk and strengthen public accountability — while enabling innovation.

Key Activities:
  • DPO capacity-building & institutional advisory
  • Privacy audits & compliance assessments
  • User-centered data governance toolkits
SDGs: 16, 17

3. Responsible AI & Public Interest Innovation

Zimbabwe and many African states are developing AI and emerging tech approaches without dedicated oversight bodies. We help ensure that policy development is participatory, rights-respecting and responsive to the realities of African communities.

Key Activities:
  • National and regional policy dialogues
  • AI & Public Interest Roundtables with Ministries and civic actors
  • Annual “State of AI, Civic Space & Digital Rights in Zimbabwe” report
SDGs: 16, 5, 10 & African Union’s Digital Transformation Strategy 2063
