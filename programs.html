Our Practice Areas

AGCIH operates as a research, capacity and innovation hub. Detailed program concepts are shared privately with partners and donors.

Digital Capacity Strengthening & Public Sector Innovation

Technical learning pathways and systems support for organisations, schools, institutions and small enterprises adapting to digital transformation. Includes digital literacy, data handling readiness and practical adoption support.

Research, Foresight & Policy Innovation

Evidence-based studies and futures analysis on digital transformation and emerging technology. Knowledge products developed to inform planning, strategy and responsible adoption (on request; non-advocacy).

Responsible AI & Data Governance Lab

Support for understanding and applying safe data practices and AI tools in alignment with Zimbabwe’s Data Protection Act and global ethical standards. Focus on safety, privacy, trust and organisational readiness.
