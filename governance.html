Governance & Board Charter

The Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) is established as a governance and public-sector innovation trust. This Charter summarises how the organisation is governed, how the Board of Trustees functions, and how integrity, accountability, and public interest are safeguarded.

1. Purpose of the Board

The Board of Trustees provides independent governance and strategic oversight to the Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH). The Board safeguards AGCIH’s mission, ensures alignment with its founding Trust Deed, and upholds standards of integrity, accountability, and public interest.

In doing so, the Board ensures that AGCIH’s work at the intersection of governance, artificial intelligence, digital transformation, and civic innovation remains principled, evidence-informed, and responsive to Zimbabwean and regional institutional realities.

2. Role and Responsibilities

The Board of Trustees:

  • Approves AGCIH’s strategic direction, thematic priorities, and major programmes.
  • Oversees financial stewardship, risk management, and compliance with the Trust Deed and applicable laws.
  • Ensures AGCIH’s work reflects its core values of governance, inclusion, ethics, and public interest.
  • Appoints, supports, and provides oversight to the Executive Director.
  • Reviews organisational performance, major partnerships, and institutional risks.
  • Ensures AGCIH’s engagement with governments, regulators, partners, and communities remains non-partisan, professional, and constructive.

3. Board Composition & Expertise

The Board is multi-disciplinary by design, bringing together expertise aligned with AGCIH’s governance mandate and public-interest responsibilities.

The Board includes designated expertise in:

  • Legal, Governance & Compliance
  • Gender, Inclusion & Equity
  • Digital Transformation & AI Governance
  • Finance, Audit & Risk Oversight
  • Youth, Innovation & Future Readiness

Additional expertise may be added as AGCIH’s work evolves, in line with strategic needs and regulatory requirements.

4. Independence & Integrity

Board members serve in their personal capacity and not as representatives of any institution. They are expected to:

  • Disclose and manage conflicts of interest.
  • Uphold confidentiality where required.
  • Act at all times in the best interests of AGCIH’s mission and the broader public interest.

The Board collectively upholds a culture of integrity, transparency, and responsible stewardship, consistent with AGCIH’s governance-focused mandate.

5. Relationship with the Executive Director

The Executive Director is responsible for AGCIH’s strategy, operations, partnerships, and external engagement. The Board provides oversight, guidance, and support without interfering in day-to-day management.

The relationship is one of trust, accountability, and constructive challenge, enabling innovation while maintaining strong governance discipline.

6. Board Meetings & Decision-Making

The Board meets a minimum of two (2) times per year, with additional meetings convened as required to address strategic or risk-related matters. Meetings may be held in person or virtually, in accordance with the Trust Deed and applicable regulations.

  • Decisions are made by consensus wherever possible.
  • Formal voting is used where consensus cannot be reached.
  • Decisions and resolutions are documented to support accountability and institutional memory.

7. Inclusion & Responsible Technology

Recognising the institutional and societal implications of AI and digital systems, the Board commits to:

  • Promoting gender equality, youth participation, and inclusion.
  • Ensuring AGCIH’s work on AI and data systems is ethical, rights-respecting, and context-aware.
  • Anticipating long-term impacts of emerging technologies on public administration and accountability.
  • Encouraging open, multi-stakeholder dialogue on governance and digital innovation.

Through this Charter, the Board affirms its role in stewarding AGCIH as a trusted, principled, and future-oriented institution.

This Governance & Board Charter is a public summary for transparency and partner engagement. Detailed provisions are contained in AGCIH’s Trust Deed and related internal governance instruments.
