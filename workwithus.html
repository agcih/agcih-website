Work With Us

Partner with AGCIH through collaborations, consultancies, internships, volunteering and future vacancies.

The Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) welcomes individuals and institutions who share our mission to strengthen governance, AI readiness and digital transformation across Africa. Whether you are an expert, a student, an innovator, a researcher, a policymaker or an institution, there is a place for meaningful collaboration.

Explore the pathways to work with AGCIH below.

Partnerships & Strategic Collaboration

Collaborate with us to advance ethical, governance-led digital transformation across Africa.

AGCIH partners with institutions and professionals committed to good governance, responsible AI and evidence-informed public sector innovation. We welcome collaborations that strengthen digital governance ecosystems across Zimbabwe and the wider region.

We work with:

  • Government ministries, regulators and oversight bodies
  • Development partners, UN agencies and multilateral institutions
  • Universities, think tanks and research institutions
  • Technology companies and digital innovation ecosystems
  • Civil society organisations and civic innovators
  • Independent experts, analysts and consultants

AGCIH engages in mandate-aligned partnerships that protect institutional roles, avoid duplication, and strengthen credible governance outcomes.

Consultancies & Expert Engagements

Opportunities for governance, legal, technology and policy experts to contribute to AGCIH’s work.

AGCIH engages consultants and associates to contribute to specific research assignments, advisory projects, training programmes and policy development work.

  • AI governance and digital policy support
  • Legal and regulatory reviews
  • Research, policy briefs and thematic analysis
  • Capacity-building and curriculum development
  • Scenario planning and strategic foresight
  • Monitoring, evaluation and learning (MEL)

Volunteering & Internships

Early-career pathways to gain experience in governance, AI policy and digital innovation.

AGCIH provides opportunities for volunteers and interns to support research, events, communications, and civic innovation projects. These roles are learning-oriented and suitable for individuals exploring careers in governance, AI policy, public administration, human rights, and civic technology.

Express Interest – Volunteering & Internships

To express interest, email: admin@agcih.africa

Include:

  • A short motivation
  • Your areas of interest
  • Your background or study level
  • Your availability dates

AGCIH keeps promising profiles on file and may reach out when opportunities arise.
