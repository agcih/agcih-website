Work With Us

Structured engagement pathways aligned with AGCIH’s mandate.

The Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) engages with governments, public institutions, oversight bodies, development partners, and specialised experts to strengthen governance readiness and accountability for high-impact digital and AI systems.

AGCIH maintains an institutional posture: we do not operate as a general consulting marketplace or open volunteer hub. Engagements are shaped by mandate alignment, governance integrity, and the practical utility of the work.

Institutional Collaboration

Partnerships that strengthen governance, oversight, and responsible implementation.

AGCIH collaborates with institutions seeking practical support to strengthen governance arrangements for digital and AI-enabled public systems. We prioritise collaborations that are implementable, mandate-respecting, and focused on measurable institutional outcomes.

Typical collaboration counterparts include:

  • Government ministries and agencies
  • Regulators and oversight bodies
  • Development partners and multilateral institutions
  • Universities and research institutions (applied governance work)
  • Technology and innovation ecosystems (where institutional governance is central)

AGCIH does not duplicate the roles of regulators, implementers, or norm-setting bodies. Our value lies in helping institutions operationalise governance: roles, decision rights, risk escalation, assurance, and accountability.

Expert Network

Specialised contributions to scoped, mandate-aligned workstreams.

AGCIH maintains an expert network to support defined workstreams such as governance diagnostics, institutional design, training for oversight functions, and applied research that produces practical tools.

Expertise areas may include:

  • Governance and institutional design
  • Public law, regulation, and oversight systems
  • Risk management, assurance, and audit approaches
  • Data governance and public-sector digital policy
  • Monitoring, evaluation, and learning (MEL)
  • Responsible AI governance in public services (non-deployment)

Inclusion in the expert network does not guarantee assignments. Engagement is based on scoped needs, integrity screening, and relevance to a specific institutional engagement.

Early-Career Interest (Controlled)

Structured learning-oriented opportunities may be opened selectively.

From time to time, AGCIH may host limited, structured internship or learning placements tied to defined outputs (e.g., literature mapping, policy scanning, event support). These opportunities are selective and guided by supervisory capacity and programme needs.
