Work With Us

Partner with AGCIH through collaborations, consultancies, internships, volunteering and future vacancies.

The Africa Governance and Civic Innovation Hub welcomes individuals and institutions who share our mission to strengthen governance, artificial intelligence readiness and digital transformation across Africa. Whether you are an expert, student, innovator, researcher, policymaker or institution, there is space for meaningful collaboration.

Partnerships and Strategic Collaboration

Collaborate with us to advance governance-led digital transformation across Africa.

AGCIH partners with institutions and professionals committed to good governance, responsible artificial intelligence and evidence-informed public sector innovation.

  • Government ministries, regulators and oversight bodies
  • Development partners, United Nations agencies and multilateral institutions
  • Universities, think tanks and research institutions
  • Technology companies and digital innovation ecosystems
  • Civil society organisations and civic innovators
  • Independent experts, analysts and consultants

AGCIH engages in mandate-aligned partnerships that protect institutional roles, avoid duplication, and strengthen credible governance outcomes.
