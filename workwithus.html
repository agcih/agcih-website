Work With Us

The Africa Governance & Civic Innovation Hub (AGCIH) is building a community of partners and professionals committed to making digital transformation in Zimbabwe and Africa more ethical, inclusive and trusted. We work at the intersection of governance, technology and human dignity.

We do not build technology.

Our role is to help institutions, regulators and communities move with clarity as AI and digital systems become part of everyday governance and public service delivery.

1. Partnerships & Collaboration

We welcome collaboration with institutions that are exploring responsible AI, digital transformation, and public interest governance. AGCIH works in a spirit of co-creation and mutual learning rather than competition.

We are particularly interested in partnering with:

  • Government ministries, agencies and independent commissions working on digital policy or service delivery.
  • Regulatory bodies involved in ICT, data protection, or emerging technologies.
  • Universities, research centres and think tanks interested in AI governance and digital public policy.
  • Regional and international organisations that support ethical and inclusive digital transformation.
  • Private sector and innovation ecosystems that are committed to responsible, human-centred AI.

Partnerships may include joint research, dialogue platforms, technical advisory work, pilot initiatives, and knowledge exchange.

2. Professional Opportunities

As AGCIH grows, we will engage a range of professionals on a project and advisory basis. We are building a pool of people who are interested in contributing to this work.

We are especially interested in:

  • Governance, law and public policy specialists.
  • Researchers working on AI, data governance, or digital transformation.
  • Digital ethics and human rights practitioners.
  • Data and technology experts interested in public interest work.
  • Monitoring, learning and evaluation practitioners for governance and tech projects.

Opportunities may take the form of short consultancies, research assignments, fellowships, or part-time project roles as AGCIH’s programming develops.

3. Volunteers, Fellows & Emerging Professionals

AGCIH also welcomes interest from volunteers and emerging professionals who are passionate about the future of governance and technology in Africa.

Examples of how volunteers and fellows may contribute include:

  • Supporting background research and documentation for AI governance and digital policy work.
  • Assisting with knowledge products, case studies and learning briefs.
  • Helping to organise roundtables, dialogues or small learning events.
  • Contributing to communications and knowledge-sharing about AGCIH’s work.

Volunteer and fellowship opportunities will be structured to ensure learning, mentorship and clear contribution to AGCIH’s mission.

4. Future Vacancies

As AGCIH’s work expands, we will periodically advertise specific roles, consultancies and fellowships on this page and through our networks. Even if there is no open vacancy listed at the moment, we are happy to hear from people who share our vision.
